ClearPuff data deletion

ClearPuff currently works without an online account. Habit logs and settings are stored locally on your device.

Delete local data

  1. Use the reset or delete-data option in the app when available; or
  2. Open your device settings, select ClearPuff, and clear its storage; or
  3. Uninstall ClearPuff.

Purchase records may remain with Google Play, Apple, or the subscription provider under their policies. For help, email buildingsofttech@gmail.com.
